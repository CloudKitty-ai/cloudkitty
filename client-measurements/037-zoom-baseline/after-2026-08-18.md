## What did not go as research predicted

**R3 said the ground bake gets smaller. It does at the large end and grows at
the small end**, and the reason is the feature working: a phone's floor tile
goes 34px → 56.7px, so the bake follows it up. 1200px is the win (2400² →
2000²); 640px pays for it (1280² → 2000²). "Smaller on every large viewport"
was only half the story and the small end was not mentioned.

> **CORRECTION, 2026-08-18, from the review of PR #246.** This section
> originally read "Nothing clamps — `GROUND_BAKE_MAX_PX` is 4096". **That was
> wrong.** The table above is in CSS px and the clamp is in DEVICE px
> (`4096 / dpr`), and every figure here was taken at dpr 1, so it could not
> have seen the clamp. Measured properly, `tile / bakeTile` at the zoom floor:
>
> | map | dpr 2 | dpr 2.625 | dpr 3 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 620px | 1.00 → 1.00 | 1.00 → **1.28** | 1.00 → **1.46** |
> | 1000px | 1.00 → 1.00 | 1.28 → 1.28 | 1.46 → 1.46 |
> | 1200px | **1.17 → 1.00** | **1.54 → 1.28** | **1.76 → 1.46** |
>
> Anything above 1.00 is an UPSCALE at the zoom floor, in steady state — which
> `bakeTileFor`'s own comment and contract invariant 6 say cannot happen.
>
> **It is not new.** Every "before" figure above is `main`: the invariant was
> already false at dpr 2.625 and 3 on large maps, and at dpr 2 on a 1200px map.
> 037 **improves the worst case** (1.76 → 1.46, because the floor tile stops
> growing with the display) and **widens the affected band downward**, from
> maps ≥800px to ≥460px at dpr 3. Memory grows with it: a 620px map at dpr 2
> goes 25 MB → 64 MB of offscreen.
>
> Deliberately not fixed here. The honest fix couples the camera's zoom floor
> to the ground cache's budget — do not zoom in past what the bake can carry —
> and that is a design decision spanning 036 and 037, not a 037 remediation.
> Parked in `BACKLOG.md`.

**R5 said the tile-denominated distance dials stay constant wherever the pixel
target is reachable. They do** — 150px of deadzone at 640, 1000 and 1200, and
varying only at 340/460 where `minTiles` binds. That is FR-008's whole argument,
now measured rather than reasoned.

## The world clamp costs the large end some range

`ceilingTiles` clamps one tile below the world, so 1000px and 1200px come back
at 19 tiles rather than the 20 and 24 their pixel target asks for. Their range
is 1.90× and 1.58×, not the 2.00× and 1.67× an unclamped ceiling would give,
and their ceiling tile is 52.6px and 63.2px rather than 50px and 60px.

**This is the Fog dependency in numbers.** On a larger world the clamp stops
binding and both viewports get the full 2.00×. The clamp is not tunable slack:
without it, camera-on at full zoom-out would be pixel-identical to camera-off
on a 1000px map, which is what 037 FR-007 exists to prevent.
