## Instrument notes

- **The poll is a sample, not coverage.** 8,507 rows at a median 20.3 s
  (25 ticks); any span shorter than 25 ticks can fall between rows, and
  one distress episode did. Continuity, alarms and distress counts rest
  on the box's own full-coverage records (journal, `/welfare`,
  `/events/distress`); the poll's readings are means and shares.
- **The client-side watch ran the whole window this time**
  (`soak_watch.sh` 300 s, 2 d 0 h 33 m at the verdict) but its stdout
  went to a session monitor that did not survive compaction, so its
  silence is not on disk. As in the Biscuit 2.0 record, the journal
  and `/welfare` are the durable record and cover the whole window.
- **Open and close are the same instrument** (2 h of the same poller
  at each end), unlike the Biscuit 2.0 record's two census windows, so
  the per-seat open-to-close deltas are directly comparable.
- `/events/activity` retains 1,000 events, too short for a span read at
  the verdict; a groom-latency confirmation on the live world would need
  it polled continuously.
