purr batteries). Deployed roster B, hearer-side per-kind deafening

**Welfare is flat within ±0.015** — an order of magnitude inside the
parity band. The purr chorus causally moves decisions (the flip-test
lineage: 6–8% act-flips) yet buys no measurable happiness; deafening
removes ~15% of purr volume (the answer-driven portion, matching the
purr-back flip rates) and the social fabric doesn't move.

**Reading**: under global vision, even the social words are
welfare-redundant — everything a purr tells a hearer, its eyes
already see. This closes the "does the chorus PAY" caveat from the
purr batteries with a clean NO at screen power, and it is the
strongest evidence yet for the fog thesis (ROADMAP phase 2): the
channel becomes load-bearing only when vision stops being global.
Registered whisper, not a claim: distress ticks double purr-deaf
(37→78, 0.02% absolute) — if fog-era ablations reproduce that at
scale, the purr's value was always tail-risk insurance, not mean
welfare.

Bounds: screen-grade (10 seeds, no world clustering); frozen minds —
a generation TRAINED deaf could differ (this measures hearing's value
to cats that grew up hearing); F-009 dimensions as stated.
