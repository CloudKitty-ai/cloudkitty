repertoire, if it emerges, must come from PPO, not the clone.

A *rough* critic, as §7's "good policy + rough critic" expects at this
stage: about half the across-world return variance is predictable from
the privileged state before any on-policy fitting. §10.1's EV > 0.7 bar
applies during PPO, where the critic trains on-policy; EV is the
watch-first diagnostic there (F-003/F-005/F-006: cooperative credit is
critic-carried).
