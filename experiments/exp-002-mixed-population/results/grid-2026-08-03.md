The other 17 pass on all three rosters. Two things follow:

1. **F-010 catatonia is alive and it is worst in the scratch
   control.** C-scratch's shape-iii mean (−0.0145) and median
   (+0.0330) disagree because three of thirty seeds collapse — worst
   seed welfare 0.1096. A mean alone would have hidden this; the
   distress-age bound caught it.
2. **The failures are per-seed, not per-cell** — M33-γ.995 fails at
   seed 2 while seeds 1 and 3 pass clean at 0 and 21. Instability is
   a training-seed lottery, not a property of the mix condition.
   Per §9.2 these candidates are out regardless of Nash.

## H3 — CONFIRMED (measured 2026-08-03, after an instrument correction)

> **Correction.** This section originally reported H3 as blocked on
> three instrument traps. An audit found that two of the three were
> one bug: `forensics_replay` labeled its probe columns positionally
> (`kitty_{j+1}`) instead of by agent name, so the candidate's data at
> kitty_4 printed as "scripted kitty_2" — the geometry was correct and
> the data valid all along. Fixed in `forensics_replay.py` (replay now
> returns agent names and roster labels separately) and verified
> against the exact invocation that produced the confusion. The one
> real trap stands: **without `--config` the probe silently runs the
> compiled 3-kitty world**, where the candidate never gets a seat and
> a naive invocation re-measures frozen s6 (~13.7%, one point off
> s6's genuine 13.26% anchor — exactly how a wrong number survives
> review). Always pass `--config`.

Screen: `trainer/h3_screen.py` — Seating-B geometry on the served
world (candidate at Kittybear/kitty_4, frozen s6 at Miso/kitty_1,
scripted Biscuit playful / Pumpkin needs_driven), continuous 20k
ticks, pinned clock, 10 seeds from the ledger's shape-ii band

Three observations. **Warm-start arms retain functional listening**
at or above the frozen s6 they started from — H3's retention story
holds even for the M0 cells, whose training-time *emission* was
lowest (0.31–0.98 meows/1k). Listening and speaking decouple:
emission rises with mix (M67 up to 3.3/1k) while listening is broadly
similar. And **C-scratch's 23.6% is not a retention result** — its BC
clone could not emit meows at all, yet fine-tuning made its decisions
the *most* digest-sensitive of any candidate; sensitivity to the
channel features is apparently easy to acquire, which sharpens rather
than answers F-012's question about functional use.

   subject at the first kitty and rewrites every other seat to

   Shape i was therefore measured in all-needs_driven company. Paired

   cell contrasts share the geometry, so H1's conclusion stands; the
   claim should be read as "vs all-needs_driven company". No harness
   mode currently matches §8's registered shape i exactly (Deviation
   2).
