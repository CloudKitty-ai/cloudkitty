## H4 — the F-010 stability gate, and it bites

Max unresolved-distress age across rosters 3 / 4 / 5 (limit 150; §8
inherits eval-suite v1 bounds). **17 of 22 candidates pass; 5 fail.**

| Candidate | r3 | r4 | r5 | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-scratch-γ.998-s1 | 38 | **17 749** | 477 | FAIL |
| M33-γ.995-s2 | **2 282** | **3 273** | **1 995** | FAIL |
| M33-γ.998-s3 | **767** | **597** | 30 | FAIL |
| M0-γ.998-s2 | 0 | **708** | 1 | FAIL |
| M67-γ.995-s1 | 0 | **318** | 0 | FAIL |

The other 17 pass on all three rosters. Two things follow:

1. **F-010 catatonia is alive and it is worst in the scratch
   control.** C-scratch's shape-iii mean (−0.0145) and median
   (+0.0330) disagree because three of thirty seeds collapse — worst
   seed welfare 0.1096. A mean alone would have hidden this; the
   distress-age bound caught it.
2. **The failures are per-seed, not per-cell** — M33-γ.995 fails at
   seed 2 while seeds 1 and 3 pass clean at 0 and 21. Instability is
   a training-seed lottery, not a property of the mix condition.
   Per §9.2 these candidates are out regardless of Nash.

## H3 — NOT MEASURED (instrument blocked, no number reported)

The shape-ii channel screen did not produce a trustworthy reading and
**no H3 result is claimed**. Two traps were hit and a third left
unresolved:

1. `forensics_replay` without `--config` silently runs the **compiled
   3-kitty world**, where the candidate never gets a seat — a naive
   invocation re-measures frozen s6 and returns a plausible ~13.7%
   (close to s6's real 13.26% anchor). Anyone repeating this must
   pass `--config`.
2. On the served config both policy slots are engine-seated
   (`behavior = "policy:s6"` / `"policy:s3"`), so the Seating-B
   geometry cannot be obtained by `--control` + `--seat` the way the
   scripted-company probe gets it.
3. With `--config cloudkitty.toml` the probe reported 20 000
   decisions for `kitty_2` — a seat the control map declares
   scripted — which contradicts the control mapping and means seat
   attribution is not currently trustworthy on this geometry.

Stopped here rather than publish an unattributable flip rate. What
*is* available is training-time telemetry (§10.1, not H3's registered
measure): final-100-update meows per 1k decisions — M0-γ.995 0.31,
M0-γ.998 0.98, M33-γ.995 1.01, M33-γ.998 2.94, M33-γ.9985 1.79,
M67-γ.995 2.34, M67-γ.998 3.31, C-scratch 3.13. Channel use survives
fine-tuning and rises with mix, which is suggestive for H3's
direction but is emission during training, not the registered
digest-zeroing screen in policy company.

## Selection status

No winner is declared. §9.2's primary (paired Nash, gated by H4) puts
the **M0 cells at the top of shape iii and level on shape i**, and
the H4-passing M0 seeds are M0-γ.995-s1/s2/s3 and M0-γ.998-s1/s3. But
§9.2's registered secondary — prefer functional channel use among
statistical ties — cannot be applied until H3 is measured, and the
shape-ii screen is exactly what is blocked. Selection therefore waits
on the instrument fix.
