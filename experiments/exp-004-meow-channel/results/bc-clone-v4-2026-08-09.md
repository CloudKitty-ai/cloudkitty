## Training (registered recipe, prereg §5)

- One trunk **197 → 256 → 256 → 43** (34 activity + 9 message, split by
  convention). Loss = **two masked CEs, summed**; legal-only smoothing
  ε = 0.05 per head over its own mask; Adam 3e-4, batch 4096; split by
  rollout (val = rollout-03 × 15 = 478,776 rows); seed 20260809.
- Ran to the 20-epoch cap (still descending gently, plateau not
  triggered — the recipe's cap is the stop). ~2 s/epoch on this box.
- **Val at best (epoch 20)**: summed loss 1.1106 · activity top-1
  **72.7%** · message top-1 **99.94%** · H(act) 0.966, H(msg) 0.176 —
  both positive, as §11 requires.
