engine (`f24e458`, stamp `412d00e2…`), family v5 (seed 20260809, 15

## Acceptance (prereg §11, all PASS)

- Widths from the engine, uniform across 60 rollouts: obs 197 / mask
  34 / msg-mask 9, schemas 3/2/2.
- Label-legality on both heads: `mask[label] == 1` and
  `mask_msg[label_msg] == 1` for every kept row.
- `Silent` never masked: `mask_msg[:, 0]` all-ones, structural.
- No meow-turn rows (v2 menu has no meow activity; `dropped_by` clean).
- **Two-channel FR check**: activity-class distribution conditioned on
  message ≠ Silent vs unconditioned, **within behavior** (the pooled
  read mixes composition — announcing rows over-represent playful
  ~12:1 and playful cats idle more at baseline, which produced a
  spurious +2.25pp pooled Idle skew; the registered question is about
  the *decider*, so behavior is held fixed):
  - needs_driven: Idle 5.29% → **4.24%** announcing (−1.06pp — less
    idle, and move rises 45% → 57%: announcing cats are literally
    mid-walk to their errand). TV 0.126.
  - playful: Idle 10.46% → 9.95% (−0.51pp), TV 0.022 — near-identical.
  - **No Idle skew in either behavior. The collection stands.**
