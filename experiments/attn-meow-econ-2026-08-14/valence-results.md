## The grooming economy explains the mixed-world welfare finding

Grooming is a cross-currency gift (being groomed services the
groomee's Bath — the exp-004 responder mechanism). At home, s3s groom
each other ~12% of the time: everyone's Bath is socially serviced. In
mixed company s3 keeps giving (11%) but s1/s2 never reciprocate
(0.2–0.5%), and s3's WantBath asks quadruple (0.23 → 0.86–0.88/1k) —
asks falling on cats with no answering habit. The spatial signature
matches: in the mix s3 drifts peripheral (nearest 2.79→3.50, alone
29.5%→35.7%, contact .471→.425) while s1/s2 barely move. It gives
without receiving, asks without answer, edges outward, and pays the
−0.94 happiness found in the economics doc. The chatterbox-groomer
is a *culture*, not just a policy — it needs its own kind.
