## 1. What is being asked

The true-needs arm fed each friend's true hidden needs to the policy
and read LEVEL with the arm fed a head's predictions, and at or below
the plain no-head policy (entry 37). One explanation: a want-call
already stamps the caller's need into every listener's row, so the
cells duplicate what the calls carry. **Does the no-head mind lose
welfare when that stamped need is masked, and does the mind that also
holds the true needs lose less?**

Not asked: nothing here trains, seats or serves. The needs-head line
is closed for Gen 2's world (entry 40, ruling 1) and no outcome here

## 3. The mask

In the observation handed to the policy, every tick and every seat,
the six want-intensity cells of each of the four kitty rows are set
to zero (24 cells of 399). Nothing else changes: the engine, the
calls made, the call's kind, recency, rate and place in the row, the
answers-me bits, and the truth arm's appended cells (built from the
engine's state) are untouched. Hearer-side and evaluation-only.

Two seatings carry the mask by name, each its mind in all five seats:
`wantmask-nohead-s1` (`p2:p2-nohead-s1r`) and `wantmask-truth-s1`
(`p2:p2-truth-s1`). A measurement: never seated, served or a teacher.

## 6. The reads

Δ is masked minus unmasked, paired by world, on team happiness.

- **R-0, the mask's reach (dump-only).** On the recorded UNMASKED
  40×40 legs of both finals: the share of decision rows whose greedy

- **R-1, welfare.** Δ_nohead and Δ_truth, each with its count of

- **R-2, substitution inside the truth mind (replay).** The share of

  then the only one that can see it.

## 8. Reading grammar (every consequence is hers)

By Δ_nohead and D, edge 0.325 (a value at the edge is inside it):

- **Δ_nohead ≥ −0.325: NOT LOAD-BEARING.** The no-head mind's welfare
  at 40×40 does not rest on the stamped need. Redundancy through
  these cells is not what explains LEVEL; act selection (T4) and
  redundancy with the call's existence stay open. If Δ_truth is below
  −0.325 here, or either Δ is above +0.325, that is outside
  expectation, reported and put to her.
- **Δ_nohead < −0.325 and D > +0.325: SUPPORTS REDUNDANCY.** The mind
  holding the true needs loses less: the cells substitute for the
  calls.
- **Δ_nohead < −0.325 and |D| ≤ 0.325: UNINFORMATIVE.** Both lose
  alike (or the truth mind's smaller loss is inside the edge).
  Minds trained with the cells may fall for off-distribution reasons;
  a double fall is never read against redundancy. The retrain without
  want-words is the only clean form; put to her.
- **Δ_nohead < −0.325 and D < −0.325: REVERSED.** The truth mind
  loses more; against substitution; put to her.
- R-0's stop (§4): NOT LOAD-BEARING on R-0 alone.
- R-2 is read beside every cell: a truth-cell share that rises under
  the mask is substitution inside the trained function; a flat one
  says the cells were not standing in for the calls.

What a stopped leg leaves unscored: a no-head scout abort or stopped
leg leaves R-1, D and the grammar UNSCORED and the truth legs are not
started; a truth scout abort or stopped leg leaves D and the last
three cells UNSCORED (Δ_nohead alone still reads the first cell). An
abort under the mask is reported with the seat's own record and is
not attributed to the mask without it. A world aborted in any of the
four legs leaves every pair, the count stated.

One seed per mind clears nothing; no recipe or roadmap text changes

## 10. Blind spots (declared)

- Frozen minds, off their training distribution: a fall is dependence
  or disorientation, and only the differential and R-2 lean one way.
- The mask removes the need's level, not the call: that a friend
  called, what for, how recently and from where all remain, and may
  carry most of what the level does.
- One seed per mind; 40×40 only (the look's coverage is lower at
  100×100, where sleep, play and drink go uncalled).
- Uniform five-copy seatings are stress reads (F-017, F-044, F-060):
  the contrast holds, the level does not.
- Hearer-side only: speakers still call as they were trained to.
