## The unskilled gradient, restated

Bar 1 was written when bugs were the only plausible entry-level
prey. With the dart in, the unskilled gradient into critter play
exists on BOTH geometries — through greebles: unskilled greeble EV
13.0 (g20) and 11.2 (g26), both over the 10 bar that bar 1 applies
to bugs. Whether that satisfies the bar's intent (a gradient exists)
or violates a design intent the bars never encoded (bugs first,
greebles as the skilled prize) is the owner's call — the flip was
pre-declared as escalate-not-fail for exactly this reason.

Arithmetic for her options, using sticker linearity (scripted
behaviors don't read stickers; the earlier sweep measured the dial
linear within 0.2, though see §Provenance for that sweep's status):

- **Unflipping via greeble reduction**: bug-first ordering on g26
  needs greeble ≤ ~28 (11.2 × s/35 < 9.0 → s < 28.1) — the "reduce
  a little" budget does not reach it; 30 leaves both geometries
  flipped. A cut that deep also collapses the bug-28 < greeble
  sticker ordering.
- **Fixing g26 B1 via bug increase**: needs bug > 31.1, but B2's
  g26 ceiling caps bug ≤ 30.4. No bug sticker clears both on g26
  under dart mechanics.
- **Accepting**: the shipped world passes every bar; g26's unskilled
  gradient routes through greebles; skilled economics are in
  corridor everywhere.

## Provenance: the afternoon raws do not reproduce

While validating against the banked numbers, the b044827-era record
failed byte-reproduction. Verified, in order:

- d06f0b4 flag-off census output is byte-identical to a b044827
  build (Product's inertness claim confirmed at census scale).
- The committed raws of grid 1 (a5e1aba), grid 2 (7e95b8f), the
  sticker sweep, and the b044827 final-config census all differ
  from reruns on rebuilt instruments at their recorded commits,
  same configs, same tick counts.
- Eliminated: engine drift (the intervening commits are docs-only,
  diffed), tool drift (worktree census source byte-identical to
  main @ e39079e, unchanged since 13:46), config drift (mtimes
  predate the runs; paths in the raw headers match), seed choice
  (the tool defaults to 1..=10, the list reruns used), toolchain
  (rustc 1.97.1 installed 2026-07-18), nondeterminism (back-to-back
  reruns byte-identical), snapshot resume (the tool calls
  World::generate; persistence is server-side).
- Not eliminable after the fact: uncommitted working-tree state in
  the instrument worktree during the afternoon runs. Nothing of the
  kind survives to inspect.

The deltas are small (catch rates within ~1.5 points, EVs within
~0.4) but bar-relevant exactly where the record was already flagged
thin: the sweep's g26 bar-1 pass at 28 was banked at 10.1 with a
"+0.1, inside seed noise" caveat; the reproducible instrument reads
9.7 without dart and 9.0 with it. The g20 verdicts are robust to
the discrepancy — every g20 conclusion in the earlier docs stands
at today's numbers.

Consequences adopted here: today's rebuilt instrument is canonical;
the four afternoon raw sets keep their historical value but no
verdict may cite them without a same-instrument rerun; this doc's
numbers supersede the sweep's where they conflict. Proposed for the
findings register on the owner's word (candidate F-028): record
the instrument binary's provenance (commit, dirty-state, tool
source sha) in every census header, so a raw that cannot be
reproduced can at least be attributed.
