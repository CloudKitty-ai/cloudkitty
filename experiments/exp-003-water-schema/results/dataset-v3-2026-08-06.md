## Regeneration

```
# rebuild the binding FIRST -- a stale one silently collects the
# previous generation's dynamics
cd crates/cloudkitty-py && maturin develop --release

./experiments/tools/bc-collect/target/release/bc-collect \
  --family-dir experiments/exp-003-water-schema/family \
  --rollouts 4 --ticks 8000 --seed-base 600001 \
  --out-dir experiments/exp-003-water-schema/raw/bc-v3
```

`raw/` is gitignored; this document and the family manifest are the
committed record. World seeds are `600001 + ci·1000 + r`, i.e.
600001–614004 — disjoint by construction from training (≥ 1e6),
in-training probes (40_001–3), exp-002 shapes (100k–320k), the screens
(330k, 340k), and exp-001/exp-002 collection (400k, 500k).

**Four rollouts per config, not exp-002's three.** The prereg fixes the
split *by rollout* but not the count; four is registered here because v3
loses the policy-generated rollouts v2 had (below), and the extra world
per config is the cheapest partial compensation. **Split**: val =
`rollout-03` of every variant, 15 of 60 (25%); every world variant
appears in both train and val, on disjoint seeds.

## The gap: three action classes scripted cats never take

**30 of 40 classes present** (bc-v1, also scripted-only, had 29).
Channel rows are *not* empty as I first assumed — **3,892 decisions**
land on `WantEat`, `WantPlay` and `WantCuddle`. The clone will not start
blind on the channel.

Empty, and worth naming because each is a real behaviour the clone
cannot imitate:

| class | action | why it is absent |
|---|---|---|
| 34, 35, 38 | `Meow(WantDrink)`, `Meow(FollowMe)`, `Meow(Purr)` | scripted cats never *choose* these; the engine's background motor emits ambient purrs, which is a different thing from the deliberate action (spec 022) |
| 13, 14, 15 | `GroomKitty(0..2)` | **scripted cats never groom each other** — only themselves (`GroomSelf`, class 12, is present) |
| 4, 25 | `RestSolo`, `PlaySolo` | scripted cats prefer company; `RestWithKitty(0)` is the single most frequent class in the set at 222,994 |
| 28, 29 | `PlayCritter(2..3)` | the third and fourth critter slots are rarely both occupied and legal |

**This is the generation wall's cost, and it cannot be paid down.**
exp-002's v2 supplemented scripted rollouts with s6-generated ones
precisely so policy-like states and channel rows appeared in training.
No schema-2 policy exists, so v3 has no equivalent — the first exp-003
candidate is the earliest anything could. PPO must discover these
classes rather than refine them.

**Flagged for the water hypothesis specifically**: `GroomKitty` being
absent means allogrooming is unavailable to the clone by imitation. Per
**F-016**, grooming is the channel through which the wet-fur charge
feeds back into water occupancy — a cat that grooms relieves its Bath
need where it stands. A policy whose only grooming action is `GroomSelf`
has exactly one way to discharge that need and no social alternative to
trade against it. Whether that matters is exactly what §9.1's split-out
grooming diagnostic will show; recording the prediction now so it is
checked deliberately rather than discovered.

## Not a finding

The class-frequency profile above is scripted-demonstrator behaviour,
not a claim about the engine or about what policies will do. It
describes what the clone can learn by imitation and nothing more.
