## The duet-shaping term (prereg §3)

Detection is state-stream only, in collect_fragment: a seat
transitions INTO kitty-partnered play when its global-state block
goes to Playing (activity one-hot index 5, column 14) with the
partner-present flag (column 16) set. Column choices verified
against global_state.rs and red/green tested: the eight-case
synthetic unit check (start / continuation / solo play / joiner /
partnered non-play / pair-counts-twice / vacant block / ending) was
driven red twice with deliberately wrong columns (PLAY_COL=13,
PARTNER_COL=19 — both "missed a duet start"), then green on the
real columns.

Shaping enters the TRAINING reward only: λ = 0.1 per start, added to
the fragment's reward buffer; the runner's episode-return telemetry
stays the env's unshaped number. Truncation boundaries use the
runner's final_states row so the last step of an episode is scored
against its own episode, not the next one's reset. Identity smoke:
the same fragment collected at λ=0 and λ=0.1 (same seeds, same torch
RNG — shaping never touches the env) differs by exactly λ × starts,
and the telemetry counter agrees with the reward stream. Driven red
first with deliberately inert shaping: "reward stream says 0.0
starts, telemetry counter says 14.0"; reverted, green (14 starts,
diff 1.4000).

**Grind guard comparator (report-only)**: the scripted anchor's
partnered-play start rate, measured with the trainer's OWN detector
over the anchor demonstrations' state streams
(trainer/derive_duet_anchor.py): **40.4926 starts per 1k
seat-ticks** (161,950 starts / 100 × 8k × 5 seats; per-rollout
1,308–2,016). Banked in results-raw/duet-anchor-rate.json. The
telemetry flag fires above 3× (121.5/1k) and gates nothing — G3's
venue floors remain the character gate.

**Sizing note, for the record**: at the measured anchor rate the
shaping totals ≈ 2% of per-tick return (0.1 × 5 × 0.0405 ≈ 0.020
against ≈ 0.9), a little above the §3 sizing sentence's "~1% at
anchor duet rates". λ = 0.1 is the frozen number and stands; the
sizing sentence was rationale on pre-039 duet-rate estimates, and
the self-limiting argument is unchanged. Noted, not deviated.
