## Readings

- **The skill moat is crossed, by the mind trained to cross it.**
  Biscuit 2.0 spends 83% of its play on critters at 280 plays per 1k
  ticks. The same seat under the e004 incumbent read 1.4 per 1k in
  the freeze-packet window — a ~200× change at the seat, from the
  same world economics. Nothing about the stickers changed; the mind
  did.
- **Bug-first at 12:1 over greebles = the skilled ordering, live.**
  SC-007's arithmetic said unskilled hunters flip to greeble-first
  (the gradient routes through the easier target) while skilled ones
  stay bug-first. Biscuit is emphatically bug-first, which is direct
  behavioral evidence that the far-spawn corpus did what it was
  collected to do: put a mind on the skilled side of the moat.
- **Three mind classes now separate cleanly on one screen.** The
  v6-trained character mind hunts heavily (280/1k); pre-bugs-2.0
  learners barely hunt (0–7/1k) — F-019 erosion, unchanged in
  direction by the F-029 correction; and scripted needs-driven play
  sits in between (the corrected freeze-window numbers put scripted
  Clementine at 34 element plays, ~39% of her targeted play, versus
  4 now that the seat runs the e004 policy). Learners don't discover
  hunting; scripted schedules stumble into it; a mind trained on
  demonstrations of it does it constantly.
- **The duet did not lose.** Biscuit still initiates 127 kitty plays
  here — the character kept its social venue while adding the
  critter one. This is the corner the arms were built to probe, and
  it is not a trade.

## The reward-tuning freeze

The freeze was declared "until actual play numbers from deployed
minds on the bugs-2.0 world (not scripted proxies)." Those numbers
now exist and are properly classified. **Recommendation: lift the
freeze with no repricing.** The economy priced bugs to be worth a
skilled hunter's time without dominating, and that is exactly the
observed behavior — abundant bug play, bug-first ordering, duets
retained, welfare at certification. The sticker sweep's corridor
holds on live evidence; no dial needs to move.

Two honest caveats: this is one 30-minute window on one generation,
and the pre-cutover baseline it improves on is partly unverifiable
(the 20,676–21,301 bank predates raw-event retention and cannot be
re-cut — presumed nonzero, never measured zero). Neither weakens the
recommendation, because the recommendation is "change nothing."

**RULED 2026-08-22 (owner): "Lift the freeze."** The reward-tuning
freeze declared with the bugs-2.0 merge is LIFTED, no repricing.
Reward dials are open for future work under the usual sequencing
(spec-first, re-baseline before any world change).

## G6 soak (opened 2026-08-23)

The ≥48h post-seating soak (prereg §6 G6: distribution-calibrated
watch, stateful distress tripwires) is running on the served world
via `attn-cert-2026-08-14/soak_watch.sh` (5-minute polls). It leans
on the box's own spec-040 watchdog rather than re-deriving streaks
client-side, and emits only on ALARM / ROSTER change / TICKSTALL /
UNREACHABLE — edge-triggered with hourly reminders, so a sustained
condition reports without becoming a firehose.

Soak clock starts from the cutover boundary, **tick 125,349 /
2026-08-23 00:04:14Z** (pinned off the box log). Note the box's
watchdog log is the durable record regardless of whether this watch
is running — `journalctl -u cloudkitty | grep -i alarm` recovers any
event this instrument misses.

Every emit path was driven red before the healthy path's silence was
trusted. That caught a real defect: the welfare parser used escaped
quotes inside a single-quoted program, died on a SyntaxError, and
left the watch permanently unable to report an alarm while looking
perfectly quiet — the same shape as F-029, one layer out.
