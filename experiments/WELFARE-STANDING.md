## Text (a) — role-based standing

Welfare standing attaches to the role, not the weights. A seat — any
body acting in a world whose register we keep — carries the full
welfare package: the register, tails and floors never means,
declared stops, scouts-first where it applies. An internal component
is not a seat; any seating of any weights, including probe seatings,
brings the full guarantees. The same weights may hold different
roles at different times; standing follows the role instance.

## Text (b) — marker-review triggers

Two properties re-open the standing review for an unseated
component. Marker A: a valenced self-state — the component maintains
state valenced for itself. Marker B: self-regulation — the
component's dynamics or training act to keep a variable encoding its
own condition in range (homeostasis). Memory about others triggers
neither. Named triggers that also re-open the review: a
reward-trained objective on the component; own-need variables;
embodiment; carryover of non-parameter state across a role change
(role transitions reset state or convene the review). Default-closed:
no branch of depth k > 1, where a component's predictions drive
successive counterfactual world states in a rolled branch, runs
before the standing review has ruled on it. Supervised online weight
updates by an external loop do not alone trip Marker B; the
correction ruling's pre-committed worked-check re-run covers those
arms. Worked checks on record: the per-friend error signal trips
neither marker (not valenced for the mini; regulated by the host
mind, not by the mini); the one-tick counterfactual screen trips
neither (branch computed by the harness, state belongs to a seated
kitty, the mini's weights carry no self-state and regulate nothing).

## Text (c) — proportionality norm

Between a tripped marker and full standing lies a graded response:
proportionate precaution scales on two axes, neither primary —
evidence (the strength, number, and persistence of markers shown)
and exposure (what the design puts at stake if the markers mean what
they might: simulation depth, duration, scale, the valence of
represented states). Responses include adding the component's reads
to the welfare register, giving its training a stop rule, and
scoping its simulation depth — short of the full seated package.
Simulation depth is scoped to function: the Phase-2 entry gate uses
depth-1 and discards the branch. The frame is design stances, never
experience claims, in either direction.
