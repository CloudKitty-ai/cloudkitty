## Why bar 1 failed — the attribution the grid was built to make

The tether rescues ENDGAMES; the unskilled hunter dies earlier, in
the APPROACH. needs_driven hunts on need-schedule from wherever it
stands: its chases start far, and the 10-tick patience window expires
in transit before any cell wall matters (catch rate moved only
39.3% → 41.1%). The endgame-leak diagnosis was right for
close-starting (skilled) chasers — hence bar 2's clean pass — but the
unskilled pipeline loses upstream. The pounce (a second chase step on
element targets at distance 2) attacks exactly the transition the
unskilled chaser still fails, and per the owner's standing ruling is
the named next lever. Proxy caveat, recorded: needs_driven's patience
is a scripted-behavior property; a PPO learner has no such dial, so
bar 1's proxy is conservative — but a gradient the proxy cannot see
is not one we may assume a learner finds.

## Recommendation to the owner (sequencing hers to choose)

The tether is strictly beneficial and proven inert when absent; the
arc's motivating criterion (open the gradient) is unmet without the
pounce. Cleanest path: pounce lands on the same branch, one more
grid run (~an hour), merge once bar 1 clears — one world change,
one re-baseline, and her "cute enough to add anyway" instinct gets
its mechanical justification measured.
