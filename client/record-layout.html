Record the layout

resizeFor has no test harness because nothing can drive it headlessly. This records the numbers a fixture needs off the real page rather than inventing them — the difference between a test that guards something and one that agrees with itself.

It loads the live page in a frame at several viewport sizes and reads what the browser actually computed. Nothing is sent anywhere; copy the box and paste it back. Run it once per device — the values that matter most (header and footer heights) depend on font rendering, so they differ between your phone and your laptop.
